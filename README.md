# 🪴 FNP Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Overview
This project presents an in-depth **Sales Analytics Dashboard** for **Ferns N Petals (FNP)**, built using Excel.  
The dashboard visualizes performance across key business metrics such as **revenue, orders, categories, and cities** to help identify sales trends, customer behavior, and growth opportunities.

The dataset covers:
- Order & Delivery Dates  
- Product Categories & Names  
- Cities & Occasions  
- Revenue per Order  

The primary goal is to derive **data-driven insights** for improving decision-making and operational efficiency.

---

## 🎉 Revenue by Occasion
- **Valentine’s Day** contributes the **highest revenue (~₹3.3 Lakh)**, forming nearly **80% of total sales**.  
- Other occasions such as **Anniversary**, **Birthday**, and **Holi** contribute marginally.

**📈 Insight:**  
FNP’s sales are **highly occasion-driven**, with strong seasonal spikes during emotional events like **Valentine’s Day**.  
Marketing and inventory planning should heavily focus on such occasions.

---

## 🧸 Revenue by Category
- **Soft Toys** are the **best-performing category (~₹40,000)**.  
- **Sweets** and **Raksha Bandhan** products follow distantly.  
- **Colors** category contributes negligible revenue.

**📈 Insight:**  
Customers prefer **personalized or emotional gifts**. Expanding the **Soft Toy** and **Premium Bundle** categories can boost future sales.

---

## ⏰ Revenue by Hour (Order Time)
- Revenue peaks between **5 AM–10 AM** and again around **2 PM–4 PM**.  
- Decline observed during late-night hours.

**📈 Insight:**  
Most customers order during **office hours or mid-day**, likely during planning time.  
Marketing campaigns and online ads should target **10 AM–4 PM** for maximum impact.

---

## 📆 Revenue by Month
- All orders and revenue occur in **February**, directly aligning with **Valentine’s Day**.

**📈 Insight:**  
February is the **most crucial month** for FNP sales.  
Preparations and campaigns should begin in **January** to capture pre-Valentine demand.

---

## 🏙️ Top 10 Cities by Orders
- **Faridabad** leads in orders (2+), followed by **Bhatapara, Kharagpur, Kota, Phagwara, Raurkela, Tenali**, and others.

**📈 Insight:**  
Sales are more prominent in **Tier-2 cities**, showing **geographically diverse demand**.  
City-specific marketing and local collaborations can help expand reach.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights
1. **Occasion Dominance:** Valentine’s Day drives ~80% of total sales.  
2. **Category Winners:** Soft Toys and Premium Bundles are top performers.  
3. **Customer Timing:** Orders mostly occur during work hours.  
4. **Product Trends:** Magnum and Exotic Bloom Sets are bestsellers.  
5. **Regional Focus:** Tier-2 cities show strong potential for growth.  
6. **Operational Scope:** Average delivery time (~6 days) can be optimized.

---

## 🚀 Recommendations
- **💘 Pre-Valentine Campaigns:** Launch exclusive bundles and offers by late January.  
- **🎯 Category Optimization:** Focus on best-performing categories (Soft Toys, Premium Sets).  
- **🗺️ Regional Marketing:** Expand reach in Tier-2 cities like Faridabad and Kota.  
- **⚙️ Operational Efficiency:** Reduce average delivery time to <4 days.  
- **📊 Data Enhancement:** Include customer demographics, channels, and feedback for deeper insights.

---

## 🧭 Conclusion
The **FNP Sales Analytics Dashboard** reveals that the company’s sales are **strongly driven by seasonal occasions**, especially **Valentine’s Day**.  
By optimizing delivery speed, expanding top-performing product lines, and aligning marketing around key occasions, FNP can significantly enhance **customer satisfaction and profitability**.
